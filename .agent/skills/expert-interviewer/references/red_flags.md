# Expert Interviewer — Red Flag Reference Tables

## Building-Era Risk Matrix

| Era | Construction Type | Red Flag | Risk | Cost Impact |
|-----|------------------|----------|------|-------------|
| Pre-1920 | Tégla (brick) | Foundation settlement, brick-arch steel floor | HIGH | Structural engineer + potential reinforcement |
| 1920-1960 | Tégla/Panel | Kohósalak (slag) in steel-beam floors | CRITICAL | 3 000 - 5 000 Ft/nm removal |
| 1965-1985 | Panel | Aluminium wiring | HIGH | 3 000 - 5 000 Ft/nm rewiring |
| 1960-1990 | Panel | Stack ventilation (strang) blockage | MEDIUM | 100 000 - 300 000 Ft cleaning |
| 1990+ | Modern | Inadequate soundproofing between units | LOW | 500 000 - 1 500 000 Ft upgrade |
