# Red Flags — RenovAI Due-Diligence Skill

Common red flags in older Budapest housing stock that the due-diligence
skill checks for.

## Structural
- **Kohósalak (slag) under flooring**: common in 1950s–80s panel and
  tégla buildings.  Removal is expensive (10k–20k HUF/m²) and may
  require full demolition.
- **Shared plumbing stacks (osztott közmű)**: co-owner approval required
  to replace.  Some buildings ban vertical stack replacement.
- **Undocumented electrical work**: old aluminium wiring (still common in
  1960s–70s tégla buildings), undersized fuse boxes, no grounding.
- **Damp / nedvesedés**: rising damp in basement flats, condensation in
  poorly insulated panel flats.

## Legal / Administrative
- **Missing occupancy permit (használatbavételi engedély)**: common for
  loft conversions and subdivided flats.
- **Unofficial layout changes**: walls moved without permit — buyer
  inherits the legalisation cost and risk.
- **Co-op / condominium debt**: check the közös költség (common charges)
  and any special assessment funds.

## Financial
- **Quote omits key line items**: many contractor quotes exclude
  demolition, waste removal, or electrical upgrades — compare against
  similar quotes in the corpus.
- **Expiry on price validity**: some quotes expire in 15–30 days.
  Lock in prices before signing the purchase contract.
